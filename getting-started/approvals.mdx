## Overview

The Manual Approval Required step allows you to incorporate human approvals
and manual verification into your deployment workflows. When a manual
intervention point is reached, the deployment process pauses and waits for an
explicit approval or rejection from authorized team members.

- **Quality Control**: Add verification steps before critical deployments
- **Compliance**: Ensure proper sign-off for regulated environments
- **Risk Management**: Prevent unauthorized or unreviewed changes
- **Team Collaboration**: Involve relevant stakeholders in the deployment process

## Guide

This guide assumes you have a deployment already created. If not, follow the
[First Deployment](/getting-started/first-deployment) guide.

1. Navigate to your system in the Ctrlplane UI
2. Click on an environment in the environments view
3. Go to the `Policies` tab
4. Add or edit the Approval policy
5. Enable `Require approval before deployment`
6. Save your changes

Now, when you create a new deployment version for this environment, jobs will
pause and wait for manual approval before executing. Approvers can review the
deployment details and either approve or reject the deployment.
